About ChillZone

A small, friendly site for the time between things.

Made for downtime

ChillZone is for students who have a few free minutes and want to relax, play something quick, or watch something calm.

Stays on this page

Games and videos open in a viewer on this page. Some websites don't allow that, and ChillZone will tell you so instead of pretending.

Private by design

No accounts or analytics trackers. By default, preferences and progress are session-only. If you turn on “Save my settings on this device,” selected settings and game progress can be stored in this browser; you can turn it off and clear saved ChillZone settings from Settings.
